Pen tool designer. Fifteen jobs across three shops, from straight corners to letterforms. Everything you press here is the key Illustrator uses.

How to work

The pen here behaves like Illustrator's. Learn it with these keys and the muscle memory carries over.

Drawing

Click
Drop a corner point
Click and drag
Drop a curve point. The drag sets the direction and depth of the curve
Click the first point
Close the shape
Alt / Option drag
Break a handle, so a curve can turn into a corner
Alt / Option click
On the last point: end the curve there, so the next segment runs straight. On any point in edit mode: retract its handles
Shift
Constrain to 45 degrees
Esc
Finish an open path

Editing

A
Direct selection: drag points and handles
Cmd / Ctrl held
Direct selection while the pen is active
Shift + C
Convert: click a point to switch corner and smooth
Plus / Minus
Add a point on the path, or remove the one under the cursor
Delete
Remove the selected point
Cmd / Ctrl + Z
Undo, and Shift to redo

On a phone or tablet

Tap for a corner, tap and drag for a curve. Break and Straight stand in for Alt and Shift: tap one, then make your gesture. While you drag, a magnifier shows the point under your finger from a corner of the screen, out of the way of your path. Turn it off below if you would rather not have it.

What the shop pays for

Fit: within 2 units of the tube is clean, up to 6 still passes. Points: at or under par. Tangents: no kinks where the sign is round. Three stars needs all three.

Workshop

Paste a path from Illustrator to turn it into a job. In Illustrator: select the shape, File, Export, SVG, then open the file and copy what is inside d="…". Straight lines and curves only, no arcs.
